Under construction. This page will be a more complicated one to finish and maintain, given the volume of data I intend to include. My plan is to use posts to log watches of Films in my library, along with ratings and reviews associated with those watches, and list those posts in a feed akin to Letterboxd. I have a record of films watched without ratings going back to around 2017 in Trakt, and with ratings going back to 2023 in Letterboxd. The total is a few hundred, which is not unmanageable to convert into pages with cross-referenced posts.

Television, on the other hand, is another matter altogether. At time of writing this, I have logged 7,601 episodes of TV across 219 different shows (with some issues in the early data that I need to resolve, which may push this number up or down). Shows will get individual Series pages under Television, and completion status can be tracked with posts, in the same way that I intend to do for video games. This should be doable, but there is no way I can make individual posts for every episode of every series. The Episode tab's content will have to be generated from a data file exported from Trakt instead. I will then need to add filters to each Series page, which will list episodes with watch dates pulled from that data file.

The last detail is cross-referencing with physical media, where appropriate. Blurays are collected under Discs in my inventory, but I don't yet have a clear plan for what cross-referencing watches of discs will look like.
