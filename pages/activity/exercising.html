This page is built from an array of posts flagged with subcategory exercise, over which Jekyll then iterates and prints in reverse chronological order. A straightforward physical activity tracker, this page collects exercise records that I have pulled from fitness app histories, old text messages, and even my Google Timeline. This of course means that this is only a sample of my world line, fragments of physical memory made possible by a digital paper trail.

Currently, all listed posts are placeholders with no activity details. I'm planning to experiment with some simple visualization next (perhaps a calendar and activity distribution chart), so to make that possible with minimal friction, I needed to get all the activities with dates logged sooner than the more tedious job of populating these posts with specifics.

Where applicable, I will cross-reference these records with Gear in my inventory, a small effort of material history.
