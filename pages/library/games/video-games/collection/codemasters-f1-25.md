  "F1 25 is a racing video game developed by Codemasters and published by EA Sports. It is the eighteenth entry in the F1 series and holds the license for the 2025 Formula One and Formula 2 championships.[1] The game was released on 30 May 2025 for PlayStation 5, Windows and Xbox Series X/S. The 2026 season was released as post-launch downloadable content that was released on 2 June 2026. It received generally positive reviews from critics, who regarded it as an improvement over its predecessor."
