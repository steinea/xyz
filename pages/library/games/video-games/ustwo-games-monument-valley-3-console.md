From [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Monument_Valley_3):

"Monument Valley 3 (stylized as Monument Valley III) is a 2024 indie puzzle video game developed by Ustwo Games. It is the sequel to the 2017 game Monument Valley 2 ... Monument Valley 3 was released on Nintendo Switch, Windows, PlayStation 4, PlayStation 5 and Xbox Series X and Series S in July 2025. Netflix removed the game from their platform on 14 July 2025. It was re-released to iOS and Android as a try-before-you-buy app in December 3, 2025, along with the first in-app purchase, The Garden of Life."
