The nameless main character of the first game — whom Hornet called “Ghost” — was a silent vessel, purpose-built to contain the infection consuming Hallownest. *Silksong*’s Hornet is agentic, has a deep personal history, and makes the choice to fight for the bugs suffering at the hands of the Citadel, even at great personal cost"
