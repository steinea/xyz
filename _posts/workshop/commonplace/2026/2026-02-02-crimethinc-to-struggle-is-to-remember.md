"As climate chaos caused by the burning of fossil fuels continues to disrupt vital systems across the globe, greater numbers of people are forced to flee to safer, calmer, lands"

"In the privileged core of global capitalist society, we do not often experience the collapse of vital systems directly. Rather, we notice price hikes, layoffs, and shortages. We see wars and massacres caused by the instability produced by collapsing standards of living and the competition among strongmen to control scarce resources"

"To struggle is to remember"
