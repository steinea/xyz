"Clean energy technologies, including solar and electric vehicles, were responsible for more than a third of China’s economic growth last year, generating some $2.1 trillion in economic activity"

"The Chinese government has been a patient supporter of the country’s clean technologies for decades now — its policies governing rare earths, which are essential components in everything from electric cars to supersonic jets, [date back some six decades](https://archive.is/o/qMCHO/https://www.nytimes.com/2025/12/31/business/china-rare-earths-history.html). U.S. policy, by contrast, has been all over the place"

"“China has a vast research effort to develop new inventions from rare earths, even as the U.S. has almost abandoned basic chemistry"

"“We’re shifting away from an oil-powered world to a much less expensive solar-powered world,” Bradsher said. “And every other country is buying more and more of their electricity in effect from China, because China has a near-monopoly on solar panels. And that’s all that anybody wants to buy now in much of the developing world, because it’s a cheaper way to get your electricity than anything else.”"
